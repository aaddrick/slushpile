## How a story earns its place here

Four tests. A story that fails any of them is an accomplishment bullet, and
belongs in `profile.md` instead.

1. **Vivid** — there is a scene. A place, a time, people, a specific moment.
2. **High-stakes** — the consequence of failure was visible and real.
3. **Specific** — names, numbers, systems. Not "a major client" and "significant savings".
4. **Arc** — setup, complication, resolution. Not "I did X and got Y."
