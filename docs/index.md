# Slushpile docs

The front door is the repo [README](../README.md). Everything past the install
and the pitch lives here, grouped by what you are trying to do.

## Start here

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): what to gather before onboarding, and
  what the pipeline needs installed.
- [Skills](skills.md): every `/slushpile:*` command, what it does, and when to
  run it.
- [The workspace](workspace.md): the files onboarding writes into your
  directory, what each one is for, and who reads it.

## Reference

- [Your voice agent](voice.md): why cover letters need one, how to generate
  yours, and what happens until you do.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Architecture

- [Architecture](architecture/index.md): the pipeline diagrams, why the review
  is shaped the way it is, how scoring and calibration work, and the rules
  about personal data.
- [Diagram guide](diagrams/AGENTS.md): how to edit and re-render the `.d2`
  diagrams the architecture pages embed.

## Contributing

Repository standards, the four gates, and the rules for editing a skill are in
[CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) and [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Read
[Generated surfaces](architecture/generated-surfaces.md) before editing anything
that lists the skills — several of those lists are generated, and editing the
copy instead of the source is the one change that reliably disappears.
